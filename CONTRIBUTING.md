# Contributing to any_core_vpn

Questions, bug reports, and proposed improvements are welcome.

## Before submitting code

Read:

- The license applicable to the edition.
- LICENSE_TERMS.md.
- CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md.

Contributions intended for inclusion in the project require explicit
acceptance of Contributor Agreement version 1.0.

Include its acceptance statement in the pull request or another durable
record linked to the contribution.

## Requirements

- Submit only material You own or are authorized to contribute.
- Identify copied or third-party material and its license.
- Do not include secrets, private VPN links, tokens, or customer data.
- Use English for new code logs.
- Describe affected platforms and how the change was tested.
- Do not assume that accepting a contribution establishes license
compatibility for a native combined work.

## Commercial edition

Accepted contributions may be used in the freemium and commercial editions
under the Contributor Agreement.

Contribution alone does not grant paid repository access, support,
commercial-use exceptions, or compensation.

## Attribution

Accepted contributors will receive reasonable credit in project history,
a contributor list, and README acknowledgements.

No contributor names are listed until a contribution is accepted and its
attribution preference is known.
